CONTROL ALL ASPECTS OF YOUR TAX BUSINESS FROM A SINGLE LOCATION

The FTX Management Dashboard allows FTX customers complete control over, and visibility into, each sub-site within their tax organization. With the FTX Management Dashboard, the ability to set up sub-sites, review returns, and obtain valuable management reports is right at your fingertips.

 

Streamline Your Tax Operation

  • Set up transmit and feeder sites
  • Review both paper and e-File returns from each sub-site
  • View and manage each return and source documents for quality assurance
  • Manage and view status of tax returns for your offices

Management Dashboard 1040 Reports:

  • Group and User Summaries allow visibility into group structure and returns by user
  • Financial Totals Report that shows returns by fee and refund type
  • Bank Information Reports include total bank product counts and return status
  • Daily Activity Reports show the number of returns that are Paper Filed, e-Filed, Acknowledged, and Completed
